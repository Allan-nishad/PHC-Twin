Target Duration: 3 mins 30 secs – 4 mins 15 secs | Local URL: http://localhost:3000

PART 1: THE CORE THESIS (00:00 – 00:45)

Screen: http://localhost:3000/ (Homepage)
Timing: 45 Seconds
What to do on screen:
1. Start at hero title.
2. Scroll down to side-by-side comparison card: Traditional Dashboard vs PHC-Twin.
3. Circle mouse around "Trained Diagnostic Technician: Unavailable".
"Hello judges! Across India’s network of over 150,000 Primary Health Centres and Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centres, public health dashboards track Resource Availability — counting physical machines, beds, and drug boxes.

However, Resource Availability does NOT equal Healthcare Capability.

A rural PHC might possess a brand-new automated blood analyzer and full electricity, but if the single certified lab technician is absent, clinical diagnostic testing is 0% capable.

Traditional dashboards show green; community patients arrive, get turned away, and preventable health emergencies escalate.

We built PHC-Twin — an AI-powered healthcare capability intelligence platform that models clinical dependencies, isolates single points of failure, and orchestrates decentralized inter-facility staff and supply redeployments across India's real highway networks."

PART 2: GEOGRAPHIC HIGHWAY TWIN (00:45 – 01:30)

Screen: http://localhost:3000/phcs (PHC Network)
Timing: 45 Seconds
What to do on screen:
1. Click "PHC Network" in navbar.
2. Ensure "District Roadmap" view is active.
3. Click blue NH-30 line to show transit popup (5.2 km, ~18 mins).
4. Point to Green (Capable), Yellow (Limited buffer), and Blue Star (Sitapur Urban Donor Hub).
"Let’s look at our live geographic twin of Sitapur district in Uttar Pradesh, built on real OpenStreetMap tile layers and national highway geometry.

Instead of viewing facilities as isolated data silos, PHC-Twin maps physical transit connectivity along National Highway NH-30 and State Highway SH-26.

Our dynamic markers instantly reflect clinical readiness: green for fully capable facilities, yellow for low medicine buffer warnings, and blue stars representing regional donor hubs like Sitapur Urban Community Health Centre with verified surplus personnel.

Clicking any highway corridor calculates real-world transit latencies, road surface quality, and inter-facility driving times."

PART 3: SIMULATING A DEPENDENCY OUTAGE (01:30 – 02:15)

Screen: http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur (PHC Rampur)
Timing: 45 Seconds
What to do on screen:
1. Click on "PHC Rampur".
2. In stress-test bar, click Simulate Tech Absence.
3. Click "Diagnostic & Lab Services" tab.
4. Show Dependency Graph: Analyzer Functional (✓), Power (✓), Reagents (✓), Technician 0 (❌).
"Let's inspect PHC Rampur. Right now, it's 100% operational. But let’s simulate a daily rural reality: the certified laboratory technician is on sudden emergency leave.

Watch what happens instantly in zero milliseconds: physical hardware and electricity remain 100% functional, yet our deterministic capability engine immediately drops Diagnostic Care to 0% UNAVAILABLE.

The system evaluates the multi-tier dependency tree: because sample handling requires certified personnel, the physical machines are halted. 28 daily community patient tests are now blocked."

PART 4: MULTIMODAL REGISTER VISION & MULTILINGUAL VOICE (02:15 – 03:00)

Screen: http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur (Scroll down)
Timing: 45 Seconds
What to do on screen:
1. In Voice Dispatch bar, select "हिंदी (Hindi)" and click 🔊 Listen Broadcast.
2. Scroll to "Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision • Paper Stock Register Scanner".
3. Click Scan Physical Paper Ledger.
4. Show extracted discrepancy table (Phantom Inventory: 0 vials).
5. Click ⚡ Synchronize & Correct Digital Twin.
"To ensure linguistic inclusivity across India’s diverse states, our voice engine broadcasts emergency alerts aloud in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, and English.

Furthermore, we address a critical rural ground reality: over 65% of rural Indian PHCs still use physical paper stock registers. Often, district databases show medicines available while physical shelves are empty — what we call Phantom Inventory.

We integrated Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Multimodal Vision. With one click, it audits handwritten rural HMIS Form 5A registers, extracts physical reagent counts, detects the zero-stock discrepancy, and synchronizes the digital twin in real time."

PART 5: GEMINI REASONING & CLOSED-LOOP RECOVERY (03:00 – 03:45)

Screen: Bottom of http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur
Timing: 45 Seconds
What to do on screen:
1. Show the Gemini Decision Intelligence card & donor safety note.
2. Click Approve Dispatch (CMO Gate).
3. Click pulsating blue button 🚀 Execute Live Simulation.
4. Point to top diagnostic badge turning 🟢 100% AVAILABLE and the green Recovery Toast (+28 patients protected).
"Now, how do we solve the outage?

Instead of turning patients away, Google Gemini 1.5 Flash evaluates the surrounding facility cluster. It identifies Sitapur Urban CHC, located just 5.2 km away on NH-30, which has 3 technicians on duty.

Crucially, Gemini enforces an algorithmic Donor Safety Margin — verifying that dispatching 1 technician leaves Sitapur Urban with 2 technicians, safely above its own local patient threshold.

Gemini formats a structured order for the Chief Medical Officer. The CMO clicks Approve Dispatch, and executes live deployment.

Instantly, the technician is reassigned via the NH-30 corridor, Rampur’s diagnostic service returns to 100% AVAILABLE, and 28 daily community patients are protected."
