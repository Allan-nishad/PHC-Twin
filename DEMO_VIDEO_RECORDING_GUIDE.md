## 🛠️ PRE-RECORDING CHECKLIST (FOR THE RECORDER)

1. **Start the App**:
   - Open terminal in project folder and run: `npm run dev`
   - Open browser at: **`http://localhost:3000`** (or `http://localhost:3001`).
2. **Browser Settings**:
   - Press **F11** to enter clean fullscreen (hides URL bar and bookmarks).
   - Set browser zoom to **100%**.
3. **Audio Check**:
   - Speak in a clear, confident, and energetic tone.
   - Keep a steady pace (don't rush through the numbers).

## ⏱️ STEP-BY-STEP RECORDING FLOW & SPOKEN SCRIPT

```
================================================================================
PART 1: THE CORE THESIS & THE "INVENTORY PARADOX"
⏱️ TIMING: 00:00 – 00:45 (45 Seconds)
================================================================================
[URL / SCREEN]: http://localhost:3000/ (Homepage)
[WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN]:
1. Start at the top Hero title ("Know what healthcare each PHC can actually deliver").
2. Scroll slowly down to the side-by-side comparison card:
   "Traditional Hospital Dashboard" VS "PHC-Twin Capability Engine".
3. Circle mouse around "Trained Diagnostic Technician: Unavailable".

[WHAT TO SPEAK]:
"Hello judges! Across India’s network of over 150,000 Primary Health Centres 
and Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centres, public health dashboards 
track Resource Availability — counting physical machines, beds, and drug boxes.

However, Resource Availability does NOT equal Healthcare Capability.

A rural PHC might possess a brand-new automated blood analyzer and full electricity, 
but if the single certified lab technician is absent, clinical diagnostic testing 
is 0% capable.

Traditional dashboards show green; community patients arrive, get turned away, 
and preventable health emergencies escalate.

We built PHC-Twin — an AI-powered healthcare capability intelligence platform that 
models clinical dependencies, isolates single points of failure, and orchestrates 
decentralized inter-facility staff and supply redeployments across India's real 
highway networks."


================================================================================
PART 2: GEOGRAPHIC HIGHWAY TWIN & REAL ROAD NETWORKS
⏱️ TIMING: 00:45 – 01:30 (45 Seconds)
================================================================================
[URL / SCREEN]: http://localhost:3000/phcs (PHC Network Registry)
[WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN]:
1. Click "PHC Network" in top navigation bar.
2. Ensure "District Roadmap" view is active (top right toggle).
3. Hover and click on the blue NH-30 Highway line to show the road popup:
   (5.2 km, ~18 mins drive time).
4. Point to the pins: Green (Capable), Yellow (Limited buffer), and Blue Star (Sitapur Urban Donor Hub).

[WHAT TO SPEAK]:
"Let’s look at our live geographic twin of Sitapur district in Uttar Pradesh, 
built on real OpenStreetMap tile layers and national highway geometry.

Instead of viewing facilities as isolated data silos, PHC-Twin maps physical 
transit connectivity along National Highway NH-30 and State Highway SH-26.

Our dynamic markers instantly reflect clinical readiness: green for fully capable 
facilities, yellow for low medicine buffer warnings, and blue stars representing 
regional donor hubs like Sitapur Urban Community Health Centre with verified 
surplus personnel.

Clicking any highway corridor calculates real-world transit latencies, road 
surface quality, and inter-facility driving times."


================================================================================
PART 3: SIMULATING A DEPENDENCY OUTAGE
⏱️ TIMING: 01:30 – 02:15 (45 Seconds)
================================================================================
[URL / SCREEN]: http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur (PHC Rampur Profile)
[WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN]:
1. Click on "PHC Rampur" from the map or table.
2. In the "Live What-If Stress-Test" bar, click "Simulate Tech Absence".
3. Click the "Diagnostic & Lab Services" tab.
4. Scroll down to show the Prerequisite Dependency Graph:
   - Analyzer Machine: Functional (✓)
   - Power & Reagents: Available (✓)
   - Trained Lab Technician: 0 of 1 on duty (❌ UNAVAILABLE)

[WHAT TO SPEAK]:
"Let's inspect PHC Rampur. Right now, it's 100% operational. But let’s simulate 
a daily rural reality: the certified laboratory technician is on sudden emergency leave.

Watch what happens instantly in zero milliseconds: physical hardware and electricity 
remain 100% functional, yet our deterministic capability engine immediately drops 
Diagnostic Care to 0% UNAVAILABLE.

The system evaluates the multi-tier dependency tree: because sample handling 
requires certified personnel, the physical machines are halted. 28 daily community 
patient tests are now blocked."


================================================================================
PART 4: MULTIMODAL REGISTER VISION & MULTILINGUAL VOICE
⏱️ TIMING: 02:15 – 03:00 (45 Seconds)
================================================================================
[URL / SCREEN]: http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur (Scroll down)
[WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN]:
1. In the Multilingual Voice Dispatch bar, select "हिंदी (Hindi)" and click "🔊 Listen Broadcast" (plays 2-3s audio).
2. Scroll to "Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision • Paper Stock Register Scanner (HMIS Form 5A)".
3. Click the purple button: "Scan Physical Paper Ledger".
4. Show the extracted discrepancy table highlighting "Phantom Inventory (Empty)".
5. Click "⚡ Synchronize & Correct Digital Twin".

[WHAT TO SPEAK]:
"To ensure linguistic inclusivity across India’s diverse states, our voice engine 
broadcasts emergency alerts aloud in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, and English.

Furthermore, we address a critical rural ground reality: over 65% of rural Indian 
PHCs still use physical paper stock registers. Often, district databases show 
medicines available while physical shelves are empty — what we call Phantom Inventory.

We integrated Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Multimodal Vision. With one click, it audits 
handwritten rural HMIS Form 5A registers, extracts physical reagent counts, 
detects the zero-stock discrepancy, and synchronizes the digital twin in real time."


================================================================================
PART 5: GEMINI DECISION INTELLIGENCE & CLOSED-LOOP RECOVERY
⏱️ TIMING: 03:00 – 03:45 (45 Seconds)
================================================================================
[URL / SCREEN]: Bottom of http://localhost:3000/phcs/phc-rampur
[WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN]:
1. Show the "District Operational Decision Intelligence" card.
2. Point mouse to the "Donor Facility Safeguard" note.
3. Click "Approve Dispatch" (CMO Gate).
4. Click pulsating blue button: "🚀 Execute Live Simulation".
5. Point mouse to the top diagnostic badge turning 🟢 100% AVAILABLE.
6. Point to the green Global Recovery Toast on the bottom right (+28 patients protected).

[WHAT TO SPEAK]:
"Now, how do we solve the outage?

Instead of turning patients away, Google Gemini 1.5 Flash evaluates the surrounding 
facility cluster. It identifies Sitapur Urban CHC, located just 5.2 km away on NH-30, 
which has 3 technicians on duty.

Crucially, Gemini enforces an algorithmic Donor Safety Margin — verifying that 
dispatching 1 technician leaves Sitapur Urban with 2 technicians, safely above 
its own local patient threshold.

Gemini formats a structured order for the Chief Medical Officer. The CMO clicks 
Approve Dispatch, and executes live deployment.

Instantly, the technician is reassigned via the NH-30 corridor, Rampur’s diagnostic 
service returns to 100% AVAILABLE, and 28 daily community patients are protected."

Built on Next.js 14, Leaflet, and Google Cloud AI, PHC-Twin is ready for pilot
```
